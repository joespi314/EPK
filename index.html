Artist Bio

Joseph Schwartz is a Chicago-based independent music creator and songwriter pioneering a new path in music creation. He leverages AI as a collaborative tool, combining songwriting and arrangement with human mixing and mastering in Audacity.

He pairs powerful lyrics—both his own and those of collaborators like Herman Bean—with AI-generated instrumentation and vocals to craft music that is both forward-thinking and deeply personal. Unified by a focus on lyrics that provoke thought, his explorations fearlessly span genres—from rock, indie, and folk to pop, dance, and storytelling.
